WHAT IT DOES

Capture

Type it, paste it, or forward it. No folder, no title, no decision. The stash takes it as it comes.

Connect

Every thought finds its neighbours on its own. Related notes surface together instead of sitting in separate files.

Return

Search by what you half-remember. Thought Stash answers with the thread, not just the note.
